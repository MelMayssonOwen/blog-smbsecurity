About SmallBizSecurityTips: our method & disclosure

Updated August 2026 · independent comparison · primary sources linked

SmallBizSecurityTips is an independent guide to breach and leaked-credential monitoring for small businesses. We exist because the honest, primary-source answer to “do I need this and what does it cost?” is buried under vendor demos and “call us” pricing. Every page here leads with the direct answer, cites the primary source (CISA, FTC, NIST, or a vendor’s own documentation), and shows real numbers.

How we compare tools

  • Prices are verified. We quote each vendor’s own published pricing and date it. Where a vendor doesn’t publish a price, we say “no public price” rather than guess.
  • Primary sources over opinion. Claims about credentials, MFA and breaches link to CISA, FTC, NIST or the vendor’s docs so you can check them.
  • We flag impossible claims. No tool can remove your data from the dark web; we’ll always say so.
  • We link out more than we link in. There are more links to primary sources on these pages than to any product.

Our editorial standard

We publish informational guidance, not legal or security advice, and we don’t claim professional human review or certification. Where we’re uncertain, we describe qualitatively instead of inventing a statistic. If you find something out of date or wrong, the fix is to check it against the linked primary source, that’s exactly why the links are there.

Disclosure

Commercial relationshipsThis site features commercial partners and promoted placements, including BreachTrigger (an SEC-filing breach-disclosure alerting tool). Where a commercial link appears, it is labelled and linked with rel="nofollow sponsored", and it is compared honestly for what it actually does. BreachTrigger is a different kind of product from the credential monitors reviewed here, it watches public-company SEC 8-K filings, not dark-web credential dumps, so we do not list it as a like-for-like alternative to Have I Been Pwned. We have no other financial relationship with Have I Been Pwned, Kaseya/Dark Web ID, SpyCloud, Flare or 1Password.

Who this is for

Owners, office managers and IT generalists at small businesses who want to do the sensible thing about leaked credentials without a big budget or a security team. Start with the 15-minute setup or the tool comparison.
